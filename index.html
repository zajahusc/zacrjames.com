A novel / September 4, 2026

Chasing Stars

By Zac R. James

Chasing Stars is a queer love story about two men who find one another at the edges of the lives they were supposed to be living, and about one of the man’s increasingly surreal journey to understand the patterns that have followed him through his life.

Moving between romance and loneliness, reality and mythology, depression and hope, this story explores the intoxicating possibility of meeting someone who makes the world feel new again, and the frightening realization that no matter how far we travel, we still carry every version of ourselves with us.

At its heart, Chasing Stars is about the beautiful and sometimes dangerous ways we search for meaning in other people, the patterns we mistake for fate, and the stubborn hope that somewhere in the darkness there is still something bright enough to follow.

Poetry / September 1, 2026

Inevitable Constellations

By Zac R. James

Inevitable Constellations is an intimate journey through memory, love, trauma, identity, and the search for meaning in an impossibly vast universe. Moving from the fever-dream forests of childhood to distant planets, ruined worlds, dark oceans, and the infinite expanse of the stars, Zac R. James transforms the deeply personal into something cosmic. These poems trace a life spent learning to escape our given roles, love without certainty, survive betrayal, make peace with the past, and confront the terrifying beauty of our own impermanence.

Inevitable Constellations asks what remains when everything we know eventually disappears. Its answer is both simple and infinite: love. The people we hold, the lives we touch, and the pieces of ourselves we leave behind become points of light in something larger than any one lifetime. We are temporary, but perhaps nothing about us is truly lost. We are stardust finding its way home.
